Context

Understanding the claims process

A customer claim flows through multiple stages—from initial registration and data capture, through analysis and decision-making, to final resolution. Each stage relies on accurate information being collected and transferred.

This project makes that workflow explicit by mapping every activity, responsibility and decision point. The goal is to expose the full sequence, identify bottlenecks and reveal opportunities to streamline the process.

Outcome

Connecting process and data

The process model provides a clear and structured view of claim handling, making all steps, decisions and data explicit. It reveals where time is spent and how cases move across responsibilities.

Combining BPMN modeling with structured data makes the workflow visible and measurable. Each activity can be connected to the information it uses and produces, creating a foundation for automation and continuous improvement.
